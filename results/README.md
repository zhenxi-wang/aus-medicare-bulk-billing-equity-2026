# Aggregate results

`main_series.csv`, `main_gap.csv` and `model_*.csv` provide the primary time series and regression outputs. `contrasts.csv` and `costs_by_level.csv` supply the equity and patient-contribution plots. Remaining CSVs cover standardisation, service mix, historical projections, model diagnostics and sensitivity analyses. `validation_report.json` records the independent checks. Statistical results are expressed in percentage points where applicable.
